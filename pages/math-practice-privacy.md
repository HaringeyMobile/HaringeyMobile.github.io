# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 25 November 2026  
**Last Updated:** 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Haringey Mobile** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use **Math Practice** (the "App"), an educational app offering interactive lessons and practice across multiple math topics, including Fractions, Percentages, Negative Numbers, and more.

By using the App, you consent to the collection, use, and sharing of your information as described below.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Non-Personal Information
We collect anonymized data to improve the educational experience:
* Device model and operating system.
* Aggregated usage statistics and feature activity.
* Technical crash reports and diagnostic logs.

### B. Authentication and Third-Party Services
The App uses the following services to support core functionality:

* **Firebase Authentication:** Creating an account is optional. When you sign in, Firebase stores your account information and Firebase UID so that progress, entitlements, and other app features can be synchronized across devices and supported platforms.
* **RevenueCat:** Manages per-feature, non-consumable in-app purchase entitlements, such as removing ads or unlocking future premium content for individual math topics. If you are signed in, RevenueCat may associate those entitlements with your Firebase UID to enable cross-device restoration.
* **Google AdMob & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP):** Display ads and, where required, manage privacy, consent, and advertising choices. For child-directed users, identified through our neutral age gate, the App strictly serves contextual, non-personalized ads.
* **Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics:** Provide anonymized usage analytics and crash diagnostics to help us improve app stability and educational features.
* **Native Game Services:** Google Play Games Services, including optional Saved Games cloud sync for progress, and Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards, may collect or store activity according to Google and Apple's privacy policies.
* **Payment Processing:** Google Play and, where applicable, the Apple App Store handle all purchases. We do not receive or store your full payment details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
* **Account and Synchronization:** To authenticate an optional account and synchronize your progress and in-app purchase entitlements.
* **Ad and Consent Management:** To show ads and respect applicable consent and child-directed advertising choices.
* **App Stability:** To diagnose crashes and improve performance.
* **Analytics:** To understand aggregated feature use and improve educational content.

## 3. Sharing of Your Information
We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with:
* **Service Providers:** Google (Firebase, AdMob, UMP, and Play Games), Apple (Game Center), and RevenueCat to provide app functionality.
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or valid legal process.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Math Practice is an educational app for users of all ages. It uses a neutral age gate and does not knowingly personalize ads for child-directed users. If you are under the age of majority in your jurisdiction, please have a parent or legal guardian review this policy and manage account and purchase settings on your behalf.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices
* **Account Deletion:** You may delete your account directly inside the app under **Menu → Privacy & Data → Delete Account**. You may also request deletion through our [Account Deletion page](/math-practice/delete-account).
* **Advertising Choices:** Reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings and review consent choices presented by UMP where applicable.
* **Analytics:** Device and app settings may provide controls over analytics and advertising identifiers.
* **Purchases:** Billing, refunds, and store accounts are managed by Google Play or Apple, as applicable.

## 6. Data Retention
Account and synchronization data is retained while your account remains active or as otherwise needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Deleting your account removes the associated Firebase profile and account data, subject to limited retention requirements and independently stored third-party records.
