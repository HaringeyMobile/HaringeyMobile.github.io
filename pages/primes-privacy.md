# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 29 March 2026
**Last Updated:** 30 March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Haringey Mobile** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use **Primes and Divisibility** (the "App"). This app is part of our **Math Practice** suite, which features a unified account system.

By using the App, you consent to the collection, use, and sharing of your information as described below.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Non-Personal Information
We collect anonymized data to improve the educational experience:
* Device information (e.g., model and operating system).
* Aggregated usage statistics (playtime, features utilized).
* Technical performance data and crash reports.

### B. Unified Account & Third-Party Services
The App uses the following services to support core functionality:

* **Firebase Authentication (Unified Account):** You may optionally create a user account to sync your "Ad Removal" purchases across devices. **This account is shared across all apps in the Math Practice suite.** Creating an account in one app allows you to sign in to any other app in the suite using the same credentials.
* **RevenueCat:** Manages your in-app purchase entitlements. It uses an anonymous User ID linked to your Firebase account to ensure your "Ad Removal" status is recognized on all your devices.
* **Google AdMob:** Displays advertisements. For users identified as children, we strictly serve contextual, non-personalized ads.
* **Native Game Services:** Integrates with **Apple Game Center** and **Google Play Games** for leaderboards and achievements. These are governed by Apple and Google's respective privacy policies.
* **Payment Processing:** All transactions are handled securely by the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store**. We never see or store your credit card details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
* **Unified Access:** To authenticate your single "Math Practice" account across our suite of educational apps.
* **Ad Management:** To provide a free experience via AdMob while respecting age-appropriate settings.
* **App Stability:** To fix technical bugs identified via anonymous crash reports.

## 3. Sharing of Your Information
We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with:
* **Service Providers:** Google (Firebase/AdMob), Apple, and RevenueCat solely to provide the app features.
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or valid legal process.

## 4. Children's Privacy
**Primes and Divisibility** is an educational app for all ages.
* We do not require personal information for basic educational use.
* We serve non-personalized, contextually appropriate ads to children.
* **Accounts** are intended for parents or adult users. If you are under the age of majority, please have a guardian manage your account and purchases on your behalf.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices
* **Account Deletion:** You have the right to delete your account at any time. Because our math apps share a unified system, deleting your account will remove your profile across the entire **Math Practice** suite. You can request deletion via our [**Account Deletion Page**](/delete-account).
* **Ad Settings:** You can reset your Advertising ID in your device's system settings to limit ad personalization.
* **Purchases:** Subscription and refund management must be handled through your respective app store (Apple or Google).
